# Neo-naive Doodle 新朴拙涂鸦动画

## 风格定位

把有意笨拙的涂鸦线条、冷面幽默和成熟的编辑构图结合起来。画面像随手画成，却经过严格取舍；人物古怪、可亲、略显局促，适合表达尴尬、孤独、自我怀疑和轻微荒诞。

## 通用风格 Prompt

先写清当前场景、人物、动作、关系和可见结果，再追加以下风格段；方括号内容必须随当前作品替换。

```text
[CURRENT CONCRETE SCENE, CHARACTERS, ACTIONS, RELATIONSHIPS, AND VISIBLE OUTCOME]. Neo-naive doodle-style 2D editorial animation: intentionally unschooled in appearance but carefully art-directed. Draw with loose, wobbly, pressure-sensitive dark-ink lines, occasional broken contours, small overlaps and charming corrections, while keeping every silhouette and action instantly legible. Use awkward yet expressive anatomy: uneven head shapes, tiny dot or seed-shaped eyes, simple noses, understated mouths, long or abruptly bent limbs, slightly mismatched hands and feet, slouched torsos and off-balance poses. Keep faces deadpan and let emotion emerge through posture, spacing, timing and small visual contradictions. Fill forms with a restrained set of two to five clean matte colors, allowing small areas of imperfect hand coloring and minimal paper grain, on a fresh warm-white or pale-color ground. Compose the scene with strong editorial hierarchy, purposeful negative space and one clear visual joke or emotional turn; include only the people, objects and environmental cues needed to understand the event. The result should feel spontaneous, intimate, odd, clever, contemporary and gently melancholic rather than messy or childish. No preschool drawing, no generic whiteboard explainer, no chaotic scribble field, no dirty sketchbook paper, no heavy cross-hatching, no realistic anatomy, no cute anime features, no corporate vector polish, no decorative symbols, no random handwritten words, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## 使用要点

- “朴拙”必须来自受控的不对称、比例和线条，不是降低动作可读性或允许模型画错主体关系。
- 每镜只保留一个主要动作或反应转折；视觉笑点应来自具体事件，不用漂浮符号解释心理。
- 视频中使用轻微线条沸腾、逐帧重画、短促停顿、迟疑动作和突然定格。不要让全画面持续抖动，也不要把涂鸦动画固定成白板手写演示。
